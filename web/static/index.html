Sign-in

A real terminal of the worker: click into it and type. Open the sign-in link, choose the Google account meant for this worker, paste the code it shows into the terminal (Ctrl+Shift+V) and press Enter; accept agy's questions with Tab / Enter. When agy shows its prompt, you are signed in: press Done below.
