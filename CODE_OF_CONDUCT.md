# Code of Conduct

Agent-Forge is a small project that wants to stay friendly and useful. This code of conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project - issues, pull requests, discussions - to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Linux, Docker or AI tooling.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people. Give feedback that is specific and actionable, and accept it gracefully.
- Assume good faith. Ask before assuming.
- Keep issues and discussions on topic; put long tangents elsewhere.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, trolling.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing other people's private information (addresses, e-mail, tokens, logs with credentials) without permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions) and when someone represents the
project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem,
open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/MatakLA/Agent-forge/security/advisories/new) titled "Conduct" (only the
maintainers can read it) or contact the maintainer through the profile at https://github.com/MatakLA. Reports are handled
confidentially, and the reporter's identity is not shared.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face consequences decided by the other maintainers.
